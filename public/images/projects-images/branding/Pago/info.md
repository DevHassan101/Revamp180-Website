overview: A vibrant juice brand identity designed to reflect freshness, natural energy, and a modern lifestyle appeal. 

requirements:

* Create a premium and refreshing visual identity for the Pago juice brand.
* Strengthen brand recognition in a competitive beverage market.
* Highlight the natural fruit ingredients and product quality through engaging visuals.
* Design marketing assets that communicate freshness, flavor, and authenticity.
* Ensure consistent branding across digital, social media, and promotional campaigns


solution:

* Developed a vibrant and refreshing visual language inspired by real fruit and natural ingredients.
* Created eye-catching product advertisements featuring dynamic juice splashes and premium packaging.
* Showcased multiple flavor variants with high-quality, photorealistic product renders.
* Designed a cohesive brand system for use across social media, digital marketing, and print materials.
* Delivered high-fidelity campaign creatives that increased product appeal and strengthened brand presence.
